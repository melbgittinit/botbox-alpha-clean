# BRIDGE-1 Media Experience

## Approved short description

BRIDGE-1 is a concept-stage executive voice agent in The Bot Stores' BrandBridge Executive Suite. It is designed to help decision-makers identify, shape and qualify high-value trained-agent opportunities, then prepare a non-binding brief for authorized human review.

## What media can experience

1. Read the visual briefing at `/media.html`.
2. Select an editorial lens and inspect a clearly labeled scripted exchange.
3. See an illustrative decision-ready brief change with the selected editorial lens.
4. Review the fact sheet, journalist FAQ, authority limits and disclosure language.
5. Print or save the page as a clean briefing document.
6. Submit a consent-based request for supervised media access.
7. Enter the private voice room only after human approval and an invitation.

## Media access control

- Requests collect only name, business email, outlet, request type, reporting focus and explicit contact consent.
- Requests expire after 90 days.
- Rate limiting reduces automated or repeated submissions.
- Requests enter the protected human-review dashboard.
- Submission never generates or implies an automatic invitation.
- An authorized reviewer may approve scheduling, mark an invitation issued, complete or decline the request.

## Press facts

- **Identity:** BRIDGE-1
- **Environment:** BrandBridge Executive Suite
- **Format:** Private, browser-based voice consultation
- **Status:** Concept / supervised pilot
- **Core output:** Non-binding, decision-ready opportunity brief
- **Commercial posture:** Scoped entry paths through six-figure institutional programs; terms require human approval
- **Authority:** Diagnose, demonstrate, qualify, brief and request human review
- **Prohibited authority:** Binding pricing, contracts, guarantees, purchases or deployment commitments
- **Audio:** Not recorded by the BRIDGE-1 experience; a temporary transcript may support a live session
