# Executive Intelligence Architecture

## Locked distinction

The Bot Stores, BOT FACTORY, Agent X, BrandBridge and BRIDGE-1 cooperate, but they are not interchangeable identities.

| Component | Job | Public position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Bot Stores | Marketplace and showroom | Accessible bots and services from $0.99 to $299 |
| BOT CORE | Store navigation and shared customer infrastructure | Helps visitors discover, choose, save and return |
| BUILD-A-BOT | Custom creation attraction | Build from a template or from scratch |
| PREBUILT LOT | Ready-to-go bot attraction | Choose a bot already designed for a job |
| BOT FACTORY | Creation, testing, licensing and maintenance | Produces and supports purpose-built bots |
| Agent X | Separate professional AI workforce/agency company | High-functioning agents and teams for professional work |
| Executive Suite | Small premium institutional room | Private entry for major brands and high-value prospects |
| BrandBridge | Enterprise concept demonstration and opportunity design | Shows what company-trained and HUB-trained intelligence could do |
| BRIDGE-1 | Executive voice strategist and conversion agent | Diagnoses, demonstrates, qualifies, briefs and hands off |

## The important correction

BRIDGE-1 does **not** need the complete retail BOT CORE before a private executive beta. It needs an **Executive Intelligence Core**: a narrower, high-assurance layer containing approved enterprise knowledge, sampler boundaries, qualification state, consent, brief generation, audit history and human handoff.

The broader BOT CORE can continue powering the main store separately. When both systems mature, they exchange only approved routing and opportunity data.

## Strategic Agent X connection

Agent X may appear as a strategic professional capability available to an appropriate enterprise opportunity. BRIDGE-1 may explain what Agent X can provide, but it must not impersonate Agent X, treat Agent X as The Bot Stores' brain, or collapse the two companies into one public identity.

Examples:

- BRIDGE-1 identifies a need for a multi-agent professional workforce.
- It may recommend that the opportunity be evaluated for Agent X delivery.
- BOT FACTORY may design or license a company-specific agent.
- An authorized human determines the commercial entity, scope, proposal and contract.

## Private enterprise flow

1. A qualified visitor enters the small Executive Suite.
2. BrandBridge offers the four concept samplers or a fresh opportunity diagnosis.
3. BRIDGE-1 conducts a private voice consultation.
4. The Executive Intelligence Core retrieves approved knowledge and preserves state.
5. BRIDGE-1 proposes a bounded pilot and indicative commercial class.
6. With permission, it creates an executive brief.
7. A human decides whether delivery belongs to BOT FACTORY, Agent X or a defined collaboration.
8. Only the authorized human issues formal scope, price and agreement.

## Minimum activation gate

Private invitation-only activation requires:

- approved Agent X and BrandBridge knowledge cards;
- all four sampler boundaries;
- consent and temporary transcript rules;
- opportunity state and executive brief;
- human review destination;
- audit log and kill switch;
- supervised voice tests;
- no unsupported relationship or results claims.

Full retail BOT CORE completion is not part of this minimum gate.
