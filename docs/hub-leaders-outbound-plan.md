1. Pastors on Point → Church / Event HUB
2. Family HUB Love → Family HUB
3. Mom Connected — Dad Too → Household / Family HUB

Family HUB Love: reunion organizers, multigenerational households, caregivers, grandparents, adult children coordinating parents, family historians and smart-display households.

Mom Connected — Dad Too: parents, PTA/PTO, youth sports, dance studios, daycares, after-school programs, caregiver networks, family ministries and employer parent communities.
