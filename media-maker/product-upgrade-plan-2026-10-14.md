## Output contract
Return a sharper lead, human consequence, supported proof, audience-relevant context, conclusion/action, three useful headline alternatives, and a ready-to-read script. Adapt knowledge level and terminology without distorting facts. Show original versus rewritten text and a plain explanation of improvements. News uses explanatory clarity and relevance; persuasion mode is separate and labelled. Never optimise factual news for deception or partisan persuasion.
